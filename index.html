ABOUT ACA CHURCH PKT

50+ Years of Grace in Pattukkottai

Welcome to ACA Church PKT (Apostolic Christian Assembly), located in the heart of Pattukkottai, Thanjavur District, Tamil Nadu. For over 50 years in God's grace, our church has been a beacon of faith, prayer, and spiritual transformation for generations of families.

Founded on the unwavering truth of the Gospel, ACA Church PKT continues to grow under the anointed leadership of Pastor Sathiya Satchi. We extend a warm, spirit-filled embrace to all seekers of divine grace, nurturing a loving community rooted in Scripture and compassionate service.

OUR SENIOR PASTOR

Pastor Sathiya Satchi

"For more than 50 years, God's grace has guided ACA Church PKT in Pattukkottai. Our mission is to share Christ's divine love, build families in faith, and serve our generation with humility."

Under the visionary leadership of Pastor Sathiya Satchi, ACA Church PKT has stood as a pillar of spiritual strength in Pattukkottai and Thanjavur District. His anointed teaching of the Word and compassionate pastoral care continue to inspire thousands toward deeper devotion and prayer.

Celebrating over 50 years of God's unceasing faithfulness, Pastor Sathiya Satchi invites every family to experience God's miraculous presence and join our growing church family.
